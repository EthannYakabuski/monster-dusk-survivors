Monster Dusk: Apocalypse
Keep the light. Outlast the night.

Monster Dusk: Apocalypse is an endless portrait survival shooter with a lantern, a dash, and a whole bestiary waiting beyond the camp. Steer with a thumb joystick while your lantern fires toward the nearest threat. Stay mobile as short waves grow stronger and elite encounters test your build.

THREE SELLING CARDS
A whole bestiary after dusk
Meet 200 creature appearances and learn six enemy roles as each new wave changes the shape of the fight.

Choose how your lantern fights
Build around repeater, scatter or piercing fire, take run upgrades, and use a dash to find the gap in the horde.

Bring a little light back to camp
Grow your keeper level and permanent upgrades between runs, with daily quests and a seven-night welcome-gift streak.

TECHNICAL INFORMATION
Made with Godot 4.5.1 Compatibility. Portrait Android is primary; a Web build is provided where feasible. Local saves support offline core play. Android services have graceful Web fallbacks. Original code is Apache-2.0; source-asset/plugin notices are preserved. Google Play Games and production ads require the owner's final configuration.
